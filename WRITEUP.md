# WRITEUP

**What was built.** An ICM meta-builder for operators who ship multiple specialists across domains. Brief in, complete specialist repository out — with audit trail and Pages-ready landing page. Every build preserves its preconditions, questions, and decisions in durable markdown files rather than ephemeral chat history. Validated end-to-end through the `funeral-aftercare-compass` build.

**The design decision worth surfacing.** Three rules differentiate Foundry from generic scaffolders: (1) brief-file dispatch with full audit trail — chats end, files persist; (2) Pages-by-default discipline — every specialist ships a self-contained `docs/index.html` for immediate public deployment; (3) integrity guardrails for high-trust domains — explicit refusal to manufacture fake testimonials, metrics, or case studies unless labeled "(illustrative composite, fictional)". Audit, deployment, and integrity are non-negotiable defaults, not optional add-ons.

**What's next.** Current release v1.0 (2026-05-12). Operator-tested through the `funeral-aftercare-compass` build. The next validation milestone is a cold-test with an external operator — someone other than the author running Foundry on their own brief. No version-feature roadmap documented; v1.0 is treated as the stable contract.
